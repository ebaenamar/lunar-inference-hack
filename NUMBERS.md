## 2. Three layers of distribution (Coordinated)

**Distributed inference: one request flows through several nodes.**

- 77 % of camera frames are screened by the on-board detector and never leave the robot; the rest go to the 7B model at the base.
- Deadline-aware batching at the base: batch 3 to 34, 48 J per frame on average against 135 J unbatched.
- Each relay runs a full replica of the 30B model; splitting a model by layers across relays would add tens of ms per token (JARVIS).

**Distributed reasoning: the same decision is thought fast on the Moon and slow on Earth.**

- Every tactical decision gets a fast answer on the Moon; 9.6 % of them, the ones the fast model is unsure about, also go to Kimi K3 on Earth.
- Kimi K3's answer arrives 0.9 min later on average (store and forward).
- Wrong decisions drop from 8.9 % to 4.9 %: 26.7 corrections per robot-day (assumed error rates in `lunar_config.m`).

**Distributed allocation across data centers: each request goes to the cheapest node that meets its deadline.**

- Tactical decisions served by: robot 7.7 %, relays 0.0 %, lunar base 4.1 %, Earth 88.2 %.
- Earth work by site: Nordics 45 %, West Texas 55 %, N. Virginia 0 %.
- Lunar base: 2.15 kW average, 3.3 kW peak, GPUs busy 38 % of the time on average.
